Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Compliance with Kenyan Law

Livestock AI Manager (LAIM) is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya). We process agricultural data solely for the purpose of improving breeding outcomes.

2. Data We Collect

  • Biological Data: Cattle metrics (Weight, Age, Breed) used for inference.
  • Farm Operations: Insemination dates and success/failure outcomes.
  • User Metadata: IP addresses and timestamps for security auditing.

3. Use of AI & Machine Learning

Data submitted to this platform is anonymized and used to retrain our XGBoost models. By using this service, you consent to your anonymized herd data being used to improve the accuracy of the algorithm for the wider Kenyan agricultural community.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption for data at rest (SQLite/PostgreSQL) and in transit (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Contact Data Controller

For data erasure requests, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@laim.co.ke.
