Field Reference Guide

Score Description Key Indicators
1.0 (Emaciated) Skeleton clearly visible. Deep "V" shape in thurls. Spine and ribs are sharp and jagged.
2.0 (Thin) Bones visible but not jagged. "V" shape in thurls. Short ribs visible but rounded.
3.0 (Ideal) Smooth frame. Target Thurl is shallow "U" shape. Ribs visible only with pressure.
4.0 (Fat) Boxy and flat. Thurl is flat. Short ribs cannot be felt easily.
5.0 (Obese) Bones buried in fat. Tailhead buried in fat folds. "Blocky" appearance.

  • Activity Index (0-100):

    A measure of cow movement. High activity (>70) often indicates the cow is in Estrus (Heat) and restless.

  • Parity:

    The number of times the cow has successfully given birth. Parity 0 is a heifer; Parity 1+ is a cow.

  • Days Since Calving (DIM):

    Ideally, insemination should occur 60 to 90 days after the last birth to allow the uterus to heal.
